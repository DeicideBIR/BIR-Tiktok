    Quick guide & official sources

    Affiliates / creators

    Use commissions actually earned. Do not use the total sales attributed to your affiliate content as your own income.

    Shop sellers

    Start from gross sales/receipts, subtract returns/allowances/discounts, and use a consistent documented deduction method. Platform payout is not necessarily gross sales.

    Separate business taxes

    This page estimates individual income tax only. It does not calculate VAT, percentage tax, registration duties, filing deadlines, penalties, or local taxes.
