    05

    Quick guide & official sources

    Affiliates / creators

    Commission income is generally business/professional income. Your correct treatment depends on registration, other income, the tax option elected, and whether any withholding is shown on valid tax documents.

    Shop sellers

    RR 16-2023 provides 1% creditable withholding on one-half of covered marketplace gross remittances, subject to the ₱500,000 rules and exemptions. It does not replace the annual income tax calculation.

    Percentage tax / VAT

    Business tax treatment is separate from income tax. VAT status and the applicable threshold should be verified against current BIR guidance; this starter does not calculate VAT or percentage tax.
